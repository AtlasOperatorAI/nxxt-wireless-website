Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is this really free?
Yes. If you qualify for Lifeline, you get FREE phone service. $0/month. The federal government subsidizes it, not us.
What if my income is too high?
If you don't qualify through income, you might through SNAP, Medicaid, SSI, or another program. Check our eligibility page.
Do you run a credit check?
No. We don't check credit. Your financial history doesn't matter to us.
Can I keep my current phone?
If it's unlocked and compatible with T-Mobile, yes. We'll send you a free SIM. No new phone required.
What if I cancel?
You can stop anytime. No penalty. No cancellation fee. No contract means no lock-in.
How do I apply?
Online (fastest), by phone, or in person at a community partner location. Your choice.
