Eligibility

Do You Qualify?

You may qualify if you participate in any of these federal programs or meet the income requirement.

🍎
SNAP (Food Stamps) Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program
🏥
Medicaid Federal/State health coverage program
♿
SSI Supplemental Security Income
🏠
Federal Public Housing / Section 8 HUD housing assistance programs
🎖️
Veterans Pension Veterans or Survivor's Pension benefit
🍽️
National School Lunch Program Income-based school meal assistance
👶
Head Start Income-based early childhood program
🎓
Federal Pell Grant Need-based college financial aid
📊
Low Income Households Household income at or below 135% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines also qualifies
