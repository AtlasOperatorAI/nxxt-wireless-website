Why Join NXXT Mobile?

We understand the importance of being able to stay in touch with family, having communication for job opportunities, and having a phone in case of emergencies. Our mission is to connect underserved communities with affordable wireless service through the federal Lifeline program.

As an NXXT Mobile agent, you'll help families access this critical service while earning competitive commissions.

Become an Independent Agent

NXXT Mobile connects qualifying households to free wireless service through authorized ETC partners under the federal Lifeline program.
